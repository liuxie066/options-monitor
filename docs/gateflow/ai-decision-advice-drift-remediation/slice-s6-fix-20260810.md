## Finding decisions and fixes

### DR-S6-01 — accepted — fixed

Advice now verifies the Candidate Engine outcome before freezing inputs. Every
SP/CC family must have a status consistent with its ranked candidate count:
non-empty is `candidates_found`, empty is exactly `no_candidate`, and the
overall opening status must agree. Market closure, partial data,
`data_unavailable` and `not_applicable` therefore cannot become a synthetic
zero-candidate Advice record.

Regression coverage includes legal zero candidates, one family with candidates
plus one legal zero family, market closure, and a data-unavailable family.

### DR-S6-02 — accepted — fixed

The frozen external-evidence bundle now carries `EvidenceIndex.evidence_as_of`,
the earliest actual usable symbol `last_success_at`. Advice reads that value
instead of the view-freeze timestamp. No successful evidence produces `null`.
The derived time remains outside semantic hashes so a refresh with unchanged
facts does not force a model rerun.

Regression coverage separates a 12:00 freeze from a 06:00 evidence success and
also covers the no-success `null` path.

### DR-S6-03 — accepted — fixed

Observation replacement now requires at least one successful pipeline account
with an accepted same-run candidate snapshot. PM, option and candidate inputs
are restricted to those accepted accounts. A failed, skipped or incomplete
Tick returns without publication, leaving the prior market generation and
symbols intact.

Regression coverage seeds a prior US partition and proves that a no-success
Tick cannot replace it.
