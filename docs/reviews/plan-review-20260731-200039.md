## Assumptions tested

1. **Ordinary trades can be recorded without predeclared Combo intent.** Confirmed as a deliberate responsibility change: ordinary open trades become independent facts, while the existing explicit `pair_intent_id` path remains a separately authorized compatibility route (plan lines 40, 72-95). Current source does couple same-expiry tagging to single-trade resolution and treats staggered pairing differently, so the proposed switch targets the actual owning boundary (`src/application/trades/resolver.py:724-831`).
2. **A Long Call-first arrival needs no durable singleton state.** The plan derives `waiting_for_counterpart` from canonical ungrouped lots and rereads the pool when Sell Put arrives (plan lines 97-117). This removes arrival-order and process-restart dependence without adding a second position store.
3. **`candidate_pair_id` is not exact provenance.** Current generation uses symbol plus raw Put/Call contract symbols only (`src/application/sell_put_call_helper.py:297-299`). The plan therefore keeps it as a recommendation key and adds account/run occurrence plus time-bounded Brief exposure and confirmed-delivery evidence (plan lines 119-195).
4. **Canonical Combo identity must bind exact ledger facts.** Existing identity construction requires one role of each kind, equal positive contracts, exact record/open-event IDs and contract keys (`domain/domain/combo_identity.py:183-208`). The plan preserves that contract and does not introduce a competing identity model (plan lines 52-70).
5. **Post-trade adoption can be atomic and idempotent.** The repository already exposes `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`, commit and rollback around one connection (`src/application/ledger/repository.py:2248-2264`), and identity insert rejects a conflicting hash for the same group (`src/application/ledger/repository.py:1899-1928`). The plan places both adoption events, projection, identity and inference transition inside that boundary (plan lines 297-314).
6. **Initial empty-to-group adoption is compatible with membership history.** Current retag detection only records nonempty-group to different-nonempty-group changes (`src/application/ledger/combo_membership.py:402-410`). The plan uses empty-to-group adoption and reserves append-only void for correction rather than overwriting identity (plan lines 316-334).
7. **Additive Brief evidence can survive mixed artifacts.** Candidate groups preserve row mappings (`domain/domain/daily_decision_brief.py:522-534`) and delivery normalization preserves arbitrary `render_context` mappings (`src/application/daily_decision_brief_repository.py:1672-1674,1738-1746`). The revised plan avoids adding occurrence to fixed representative identity fields, requires unique canonical-contract back-reference, and treats missing legacy evidence as structural-only (plan lines 133-183).
8. **Multi-account rollout must be isolated.** The plan uses lowercase account-scoped modes with a production default fixed to `off`; confirmation derives and validates the account from the inference (plan lines 336-352). It does not infer safety for one account from another.

## Special review lenses

### Architecture boundary review

Pass. Pure matching policy stays in `domain/domain/`; application code owns artifact/time/repository orchestration; ledger commands own canonical writes; CLI remains an adapter. The plan reuses trade events, projection, Combo identity and membership as sources of truth and adds only an inference/audit table, not a parallel position model (plan lines 46-70, 197-226, 458-467).

### Best-practice review

Pass. The plan is fail-closed on missing time, market, quantity, contract or evidence; separates post-commit inference failure from successful trade intake; specifies deterministic IDs, stale-input hashes, transactional revalidation, fault injection, idempotent retries, append-only correction and account-scoped rollout (plan lines 85-115, 262-334, 391-445).

### Optimal-solution review

Pass. Three alternatives were challenged:

- pre-trade intent remains incompatible with the stated user flow;
- similarity/score-based automatic grouping cannot prove a specific lot pair;
- making every possible combination a durable position entity would duplicate ledger authority.

The selected path—derive a global matching from canonical open lots, retain evidence grades, and require exact user confirmation—is the smallest safe design that handles both arrival orders and ambiguity.

### Overengineering review

Pass. V1 deliberately excludes partial/multi-lot, rolling, cross-day funding, automatic confirmation, notification UI, migrations of historical groups and third-party graph dependencies (plan lines 30-40, 212-220, 447-467). Candidate evidence uses existing candidate rows and delivery `render_context` rather than a new sidecar or new delivery schema.

### Overcoupling review

Pass. Candidate occurrence does not replace coarse Daily Brief identity or delivery dedupe. Reconciler reads frozen evidence through the Daily Brief repository rather than raw path traversal. Trade intake only schedules a best-effort post-commit reconcile; canonical adoption remains a separate user-authorized ledger command. Slice boundaries allow matcher/persistence, evidence, writes and intake cutover to be reviewed independently (plan lines 173-181, 275-314, 354-389).

### Execution semantics and state-machine review

Pass. The plan defines all V1 states and terminal transitions, rejects stale confirmation with an explicit input snapshot, leaves unresolved proposals unclaimed so alternatives remain selectable, serializes competing confirms, and gives confirmed identities a single append-only supersession path (plan lines 224-334). Candidate validity at trade time is correctly separated from the 24-hour user decision window (plan lines 262-273).

### Sequencing and validation review

Pass. Slice 1 proves the pure matcher before any canonical write; Slice 2 proves evidence compatibility; Slice 3 proves transactional writes; Slice 4 performs the resolver cutover and retry integration together. The validation matrix includes order invariance, maximum-matching ambiguity, legacy artifacts, multi-process claim contention, rollback fault injection, backfill batching, receipt isolation, config validation and full regression tests (plan lines 354-445).

## Findings

No material findings.

## Open questions

No blocking open questions. Product choices that would change the V1 contract—automatic confirmation, cross-day matching, partial/multi-lot allocation or interactive notification UI—are explicitly deferred and require separate plans.

## Residual risks and tracking

| Residual risk | Why it remains | Required tracking destination |
|---|---|---|
| Same-market-date and 24-hour limits can produce false negatives for legitimate slower combinations. | This is an intentional V1 safety boundary, not an inference error. | Post-V1 product backlog; only reconsider with observed rejected/manual-pair evidence. |
| CLI-only confirmation may be less discoverable than an interactive prompt. | Notification/UI work is explicitly out of scope and adding it would widen the authorization surface. | Separate UX plan after the inference false-positive rate is measured in `observe`. |
| Additive artifact compatibility is source-supported but still needs executable proof against frozen old/new fixtures. | Digest and delivery normalization are operationally sensitive. | Slice 2 compatibility tests and binary-rollback gate before release. |
| Forced-edge evaluation repeats matching and needs a bounded performance check on unusually dense same-symbol/day pools. | No continuous score or external graph dependency is allowed; correctness takes priority. | Slice 1 benchmark/property test with a documented maximum observed and synthetic dense pool. |
| `observe` quality for one account cannot establish safety for another account or market. | Candidate population and trade patterns differ. | Account/market-specific rollout evidence; separate approval for every `confirm` enablement. |

## Final plan review conclusion

`pass-with-risks`

The plan may proceed to implementation as four scoped slices. Implementation must not silently relax the exact-two-lot boundary, add auto-confirmation, treat `candidate_pair_id` as canonical proof, or combine release/deployment with source delivery.
