## Findings

### 1 — fixed — high — current or post-expiry quotes could become historical outcome evidence

- **Path**: `mark_shadow_replay_dataset()` -> close mark -> settlement.
- **Failure**: a manually supplied `--as-of` labeled a timestamp-less current
  required-data quote as historical, and any date after expiration could be
  classified as an expiry mark. Both paths could introduce hindsight into a
  usable outcome.
- **Fix**: record mark-time and quote-source provenance; only an actual-time
  fresh OpenD collection is `verified_fresh_collection`; settlement rejects
  unverified marks. Expiry classification now requires the exact expiration
  calendar date.
- **Coverage**: exact windows, operator-asserted rejection, and post-expiry spot
  rejection.

### 2 — fixed — high — assignment P&L could be full-lifecycle or reused across episodes

- **Path**: canonical lifecycle input -> `_terminal_from_lifecycle()`.
- **Failure**: `lifecycle_pnl_net` could be used despite including pre-decision
  P&L. Even a field named as decision-time incremental could be reused for every
  episode of the same lot without proving which observation it was sliced for.
- **Fix**: remove full-lifecycle fallback. Numeric assignment/called-away
  results require `lifecycle_pnl_after_decision` or
  `incremental_pnl_after_decision` plus an exact `episode_id` or
  `decision_observed_at_utc` binding. Otherwise only action/willingness alignment
  is retained and money remains inconclusive.
- **Coverage**: full-lifecycle-only, unbound incremental, bound incremental, Put
  assignment, and Call called-away fixtures.

### 3 — fixed — medium — lifecycle quantity mismatch could misallocate event fees

- **Path**: lifecycle event -> future close/assignment outcome.
- **Failure**: an event with more contracts than the decision lot passed the
  old lower-bound check, then applied the full event fee to the smaller episode.
- **Fix**: require exact event/episode contract quantity for numeric lifecycle
  outcomes; partial or oversized events remain inconclusive instead of
  recreating the ledger allocation state machine.
- **Coverage**: mismatched-quantity lifecycle regression.

### 4 — fixed — medium — evidence-free reruns could downgrade a settled terminal fact

- **Path**: `_merge_close_outcomes()`.
- **Failure**: after a usable terminal result was written with a lifecycle
  source, rerunning settlement without that source overwrote it with
  `lifecycle_or_expiry_fact_missing`.
- **Fix**: preserve an existing usable row only against a later absence-of-input
  result. New contradictory/ambiguous evidence can still replace it with an
  inconclusive result.
- **Coverage**: lifecycle settlement followed by a no-lifecycle rerun.

### 5 — fixed — medium — material episode identity omitted outcome economics

- **Path**: close episode fingerprint/deduplication.
- **Failure**: contract quantity or multiplier could change the counterfactual
  result without necessarily changing the S3a material fingerprint.
- **Fix**: include normalized decision economics in the fingerprint and reuse
  that exact payload in the episode.
- **Coverage**: same-day observations with identical policy facts but different
  contract quantities produce separate material fingerprints.
