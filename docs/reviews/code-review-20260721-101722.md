## Change Intent

Expose existing close-advice decision facts in the Daily Decision Brief holding section without changing close policy, action identity, material-diff rules, delivery state, or production side effects.

## Entry-Point and Data-Flow Review

Reviewed the real path end to end:

```text
close_advice.csv
-> _load_close_advice()
-> _position_view()
-> normalize_daily_decision_brief()
-> build_daily_brief_user_view()
-> _position_views()
-> _position_close_details()
-> _render_user_view()
```

- The service copies only three allowlisted calculated values and does not recalculate policy or P&L.
- The canonical domain normalizer preserves position mappings, so the nested metrics survive without a schema-version change.
- The renderer gates details on both usable status and an explicit close-action allowlist.
- Hold and unavailable rows cannot expose stale metrics through this path.
- Non-finite and malformed values are discarded through `_number()` rather than rendered.
- The signed net P&L field uses `预计锁定收益` only for non-negative values and `预计平仓损益` for negative values.
- Market-qualified formatting produces `HK$` for HK and `$` for US; negative signs precede the currency marker.
- The `(mid)` qualifier prevents the reference quote from being presented as a fill guarantee.

## Adversarial Failure Pass

Checked these failure scenarios:

1. Missing `metrics`: compatibility fallback remains the previous one-line position rendering.
2. Partial metrics: only finite available facts are rendered.
3. `nan`, infinity, or malformed strings: omitted without leaking raw values.
4. Stale metrics attached to hold rows: suppressed by close-action allowlist.
5. Stale metrics attached to unavailable rows: suppressed by status gate.
6. Negative realized P&L: rendered with loss-neutral wording and correct sign.
7. Long-call sell actions: neutral `参考平仓价` wording remains directionally correct.
8. Unknown close action: no detail line, preserving fail-closed presentation.
9. Ordinary price or yield movement: action IDs and material-diff logic are untouched, so it does not independently trigger delivery.
10. Internal data leakage: projection remains allowlisted; no lot IDs, paths, broker codes, or arbitrary CSV columns enter the detail line.
