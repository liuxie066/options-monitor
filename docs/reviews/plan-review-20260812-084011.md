## Adversarial checks

### Authority and partial failure

- A missing owner cannot self-hide because v2 expected items carry the owner and the manifest binds the whole set.
- A crash before terminal manifest publication cannot feed partial candidates to current-run Brief/Advice/research/replay.
- A completed scan with no applicable owner gets an explicit empty terminal commit rather than a false missing-snapshot warning.
- Combo-only, CC+LP-only, opening-only, mixed-owner, multi-account, and interrupted cases have named tests.

### Schema and semantic preservation

- Combo v2 stores only the unique calculation/underwriting/diagnostic/rank facts needed to replace CSV; it does not add
  ranking policy or a generic artifact registry.
- CC+LP v2 is deliberately minimal: dependencies and scope status are added while ranked-pair semantics stay unchanged.
- Frozen-input parity makes any eligibility, reason, count, rank, scope status, or notification change a defect.

### Migration and rollback safety

- S1 is additive and retains v1/CSV outputs for old consumers.
- S2 removes candidate CSV consumption while compatibility producers still exist, allowing sentinel proof and a safe
  rollback to S1.
- S3 deletes producers/config/flags only after S2 proves no consumer opens the bytes.
- Historical artifacts are classified/read-only and never rewritten or synthesized into sealed authority.

### Public and operations boundary

- Required-data, Close Advice, symbols summary, mark/outcome, and unrelated CSV contracts are explicitly excluded.
- `output_mode` removal uses the existing generated-config freshness and controlled rebuild boundary; emergency stale
  override cannot bypass removed-field validation.
- Merge, release, deployment, runtime mutation, notification, and historical deletion remain outside authorization.
