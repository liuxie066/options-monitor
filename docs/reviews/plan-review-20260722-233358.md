## Assumptions tested

1. The resolved fetch window is a planner output, not the raw config maximum.
2. The production `lx` context reaches `total_cny` rather than `base_cny` and has enough capacity for TCOM 35P/40P.
3. Current production account execution is sequential, while the design must still prevent account context from producing different plans if concurrency is enabled later.
4. A shared-run coverage fixture can distinguish an account-specific refetch from a fixture that is merely missing spot, expiration edges, strike edges, or realized volatility.
5. Removing the duplicated Put cash authority is safer and smaller than teaching the fetch layer the final CNY capacity policy.
6. Request expansion can be contained by a measurable release gate without adding optimization or new runtime controls to this fix.

## Special lens results

### Architecture boundary review

Pass. The revised plan assigns market visibility to required-data planning and account eligibility to the existing cash-capacity boundary. It explicitly forbids planner and collateral-policy changes and does not introduce cross-layer cash conversion into fetching.

### Best-practice review

Pass. The revised plan now uses deterministic production-shaped fixtures, negative fail-closed cases, exact resolved-plan assertions, both sequential account orders, a bounded concurrency assertion, observable rollout metrics, and explicit stop conditions.

### Optimal-solution review

Pass. Removing the early Put cap is more maintainable than converting it to total CNY because the latter would preserve two policy authorities. Retaining the configured/spot window plus the existing final capacity gate is the smallest root-boundary fix.

### Overengineering review

Pass. The plan adds no abstraction, lock, config, schema, state, migration, or new public surface. The shared-run test and performance evidence are proportional to a production multi-account defect and do not authorize a concurrency redesign.

### Overcoupling review

Pass. Production changes are restricted to the prefilter/caller and deletion of its orphan helper. Planner, coverage, cash enrichment and risk-capacity modules are validated but not coupled through new production dependencies.
