## Assumptions tested

1. Strengthening only the final freeze boundary is sufficient to prevent invalid PM/option sources from influencing an Advice run.
2. The existing preparation owners already provide one mapped PM distribution and one account-sliced option context per OM account.
3. A shared SQLite ledger can be tested without changing its schema or introducing a second data owner.
4. Missing or mismatched strategic context remains a soft Advice gap and does not block Candidate Engine or the normal receipt.
5. The proposed two slices are small enough to review independently and do not require a new context assembly layer.

The review explicitly applied architecture-boundary, project-best-practice, optimal-solution, overengineering, and overcoupling lenses. The preparation and projection slices are otherwise appropriately narrow; no new entity, provider, persistence model, or fallback is justified.

## Findings

### PR-01-未修复-高-计划遗漏了 freeze 之前的证据范围读取，错误来源仍会改变 Advice 输入哈希
- **位置**: `Slice S1 — Final input authority contract`、`Affected files and ownership`
- **问题类型**: 架构边界 / 契约缺失 / 测试缺口
- **当前写法**: 计划只在 `src/application/ai_decision_advice/contexts.py` 的 final freeze owner 校验 prepared schema/provider/source，并断言错误来源不能取得 Advice 数据权威。
- **反例/失败场景**: 一个非 PM 的组合 payload 或非 prepared 的期权 context 带有与 candidate snapshot 相同的 run/account/config。新 freeze 校验会在稍后拒绝它，但 `run_or_reuse_ai_decision_advice()` 已先调用 `_relevant_symbols()`；该函数仍只检查 run/account/config/status，就会把错误来源中的股票加入 `freeze_evidence_index()`。
- **为什么有问题**: `freeze_external_evidence()` 虽然后续只呈现 final freeze 认可的 symbol 行，却把整个预先构建的 `EvidenceIndex.index_hash()` 写入 frozen external evidence。错误来源因此仍可改变模型输入、input binding、reuse identity 或通知 materiality，违反“错误来源即使 bindings 相同也不能取得 Advice 数据权威”的验收条件。
- **直接证据**: `src/application/ai_decision_advice/orchestration.py:132-151` 在 `build_frozen_inputs()` 前按 `_relevant_symbols()` 冻结 evidence；`orchestration.py:282-335` 对 PM/option 仅做基本 authority/status 检查；`src/application/ai_decision_advice/contexts.py:359-367` 将 `index.index_hash()` 放入 frozen evidence；`src/application/ai_decision_advice/evidence_store.py:127-137` 的 `index_hash()` 覆盖 index 中全部 symbol view。
- **影响**: 实施完成后仍存在来源漂移；负向测试可能只断言资产/期权行为空而漏掉 external evidence hash 的变化，导致 review 错误验收。
- **建议改法和验证点**: 把 pre-freeze evidence symbol derivation 纳入 S1 和 affected files。不要新增第二套来源规则；让 `_relevant_symbols()` 复用与 final freeze 相同的 prepared-authority 判定，或先得到一个仅由已验证输入派生的 symbol set。增加回归：同一 candidate 下分别注入合法 prepared 与 schema/provider/source 非法 payload，非法 payload 不得新增 evidence view，且与把该 payload 视为 unavailable 时的 frozen external evidence/hash 完全一致；合法 PM/option symbol 仍进入 evidence scope。
- **修复风险（低/中/高）**: 中
- **严重程度（低/中/高/严重）**: 高
