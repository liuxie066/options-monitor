### S2-DR-01-未修复-高-strong underwriting 会把 observe thesis 错误升级为 close
- **入口/函数**: `evaluate_close_policy()` -> `_profile_aware_policy_result()` -> `_underwriting_policy_result()`
- **文件(行号)**: `domain/domain/close_advice.py:1190-1219`; `docs/gateflow/close-advice-strategy-optimization-plan-20260723.md:238-246`
- **输入场景**: P2 underwriting short option has `tier=strong`, usable execution/fee/net evidence, `continued_willingness=true`, and `thesis_status=observe` because an IV/RV, delta, or event observation is active.
- **实际分支**: `thesis_incomplete` is false for both `valid` and `observe`; the strong branch therefore reaches unconditional `RECOMMENDATION_CLOSE`.
- **预期行为**: The accepted plan defines `observe` as a state that requests review at most. Only `valid` thesis plus complete willingness/execution evidence may reach strong close.
- **实际行为**: An observation that must not infer an order becomes a directional close recommendation in the shadow P2 result.
- **直接证据**: The implementation classifies `observe` as complete at lines 1192 and 1205-1219, while the accepted normalized-state contract says `observe` “requests review at most” at plan lines 242-244.
- **影响**: P2 replay would overstate close actions in the risk/thesis scenarios intended to stay advisory, biasing later paired evidence and any promotion decision.
- **建议改法和验证点**: Require `thesis_status=valid` for strong close; map `observe` to `review` with `review_required`; keep missing thesis as `review` with `partial`; update the strong-observe truth-table test.
