# Plan Review — S1 Receipt-Commit Scope Amendment

## Reviewed target and scope

- Target: `docs/gateflow/required-data-multi-account-integrity/s1-receipt-commit-scope-amendment.md`
- Coordinating plan section: `docs/gateflow/required-data-multi-account-integrity/plan.md` — S1 allowlist and receipt-commit addition
- Review boundary: only the owner-boundary addition for an optional
  `before_receipt_commit` callback in `publish_source_receipt()`, required-data
  quote TTL wiring, and the directly corresponding generic/quote tests
- Explicitly excluded: the other S1 provider, seal, snapshot, gateway, and
  multi-account changes; implementation fixes; commits; release/deployment

## Goal and success signal reviewed

The amendment closes the remaining payload-first / receipt-last freshness
window: a required-data quote observation that expires while its immutable
payload is being published must not receive a completion receipt. Failure may
leave an orphan immutable payload, because authority is granted only by a valid
receipt. Existing producers that omit the optional callback must preserve their
current behavior.

The stated success signals are sufficiently specific: callback placement is
after payload publication and complete receipt validation/serialization but
before receipt commit; callback failure propagates with zero receipt; quote
publication rechecks aggregate and child-request timestamps against a newly
sampled production wall clock; exact committed receipt re-entry remains owned
by the quote-layer adoption path.

## Assumptions tested

1. **The generic receipt publisher is the owning boundary.** Confirmed.
   `publish_source_receipt()` owns the payload-first write, receipt construction,
   receipt validation/serialization, and final receipt write. A quote-only check
   before entering it cannot close the interval between the two durable writes.
2. **The extension can remain optional and backward compatible.** Confirmed.
   The proposed parameter is keyword-only, appended with a `None` default; all
   existing non-quote production callers can omit it and retain the same payload
   and receipt bytes.
3. **Receipt-last failure is recoverable without cleanup.** Confirmed for the
   declared authority model. On callback rejection, the immutable payload may
   exist but no receipt exists. A matching retry can verify/reuse the payload;
   an orphan alone is not consumable authority.
4. **Exact committed re-entry does not need to rerun the callback.** Confirmed
   for the only planned callback consumer. Required-data quote publication
   resolves and adopts an exact same-run receipt before invoking the generic
   publisher; conflicts fail closed.
5. **Exception semantics are safe.** Confirmed. The amendment requires callback
   exceptions to propagate and forbids receipt publication after rejection. It
   does not add retries or exception translation that could conceal expiration.
6. **The test plan can distinguish a real TTL-boundary check from an earlier
   precheck.** Confirmed, with an implementation-review caution below: the test
   must make the pre-publication clock sample fresh and the commit-boundary clock
   sample stale, and assert one orphan payload plus zero receipts. Merely forcing
   the validator to raise whenever an orphan payload is visible proves callback
   ordering but not fresh wall-clock resampling.

## Special review lenses

### Architecture boundary

The amendment changes the narrow owner that can actually guarantee the required
ordering. Quote-specific freshness policy remains in
`opend_symbol_outputs.py`; the generic receipt module receives only a validation
hook and therefore does not import or encode required-data policy. Dependency
direction and receipt schema remain unchanged.

### Best practice

Payload-first / receipt-last publication with a final fail-closed validator is a
reasonable local durability pattern. The plan explicitly covers exception
propagation, orphan recovery, deterministic injected time, old-call-site
compatibility, and negative-path tests.

### Optimal solution

Moving the final TTL check to the receipt owner is the smallest credible fix.
A quote-only precheck leaves the time-of-check/time-of-use window open; a new
filesystem transaction, lock service, timestamp schema, or cleanup workflow is
not required to establish local receipt authority.

### Overengineering

No new schema, wrapper layer, state machine, recovery service, or distributed
coordination mechanism is introduced. One optional callback is proportionate to
the owner-boundary need.

### Overcoupling

The generic publisher is not coupled to quote types or TTL constants. Other
producers remain independent because they omit the optional callback. The quote
layer supplies its own policy validator and retains responsibility for exact
same-run adoption.

## Residual risks and tracking

- `publish_source_receipt()` remains a local filesystem protocol rather than a
  multi-process transaction. Concurrent-writer guarantees and distributed
  locking are pre-existing concerns and remain outside this amendment; track
  them as a separate receipt-storage hardening work unit if concurrent writers
  become supported.
- The generic callback contract relies on validators being side-effect-free.
  The planned required-data validator ignores the supplied receipt mapping, so
  this does not affect S1. If another producer later adopts the hook, its
  mutation and re-entry semantics need an explicit contract and focused tests;
  track that expansion in the adopting work unit rather than generalizing S1.
- During implementation review, require the quote regression to demonstrate a
  later production clock sample crossing TTL, not only callback invocation.
  This is an acceptance clarification for the stated validation, not a reason
  to broaden the plan.

## Conclusion

`pass-with-risks`

The scope amendment is code-generation-ready and is the minimal owner-correct
way to close the local receipt-commit TTL window. No material plan change is
required before implementation. Preserve the stated narrow callback contract,
and make the TTL-crossing regression prove fresh production-clock resampling at
the final boundary.
