## Assumptions Re-tested

1. Evidence identities cannot reuse account/side-bearing ledger position identity。
2. Current open-option and assigned-stock marks can be collected without mutating report state, while explicit capture can create replayable history。
3. Historical reads remain deterministic and never initialize or mutate evidence schema。
4. Assigned-stock facts are reachable through the legal application ledger boundary。
5. Agent/CLI/assistant consumers have exact scope, defaults, aggregation and test ownership。
6. Capital efficiency remains valid for intraday state transitions and assignment handoff。
7. Import/capture correction chains are atomic, idempotent and recoverable。
8. New abstractions are required by current semantics rather than speculative generalization。

## Best-practice / Optimal-solution / Overengineering / Overcoupling Review

- **Best practice**: pure domain math remains separate from application I/O and SQLite；explicit no-write reads and transactional writes match the repository's operations-sensitive contract。
- **Optimal solution**: reusing canonical ledger projection, existing OpenD adapters and existing local-write flags is safer and smaller than a second lifecycle projector or new quote service。
- **Overengineering**: general stock inventory, NAV/margin return, materialized performance and external PM changes remain excluded；the focused evidence collector is justified by external-I/O ownership。
- **Overcoupling**: option lifecycle, assigned stock, evidence persistence, public adapters and bridges have explicit owners and slice boundaries；consumer migration does not move business logic into assistant/CLI layers。
