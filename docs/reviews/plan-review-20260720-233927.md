## Goal confirmation

The confirmed work unit is to replace the system-shaped Daily Decision Brief notification with a compact user projection while preserving the canonical brief, diff, repository, delivery confirmation, scheduler cadence, strategy engines, and audit facts. Success means the 09:40 scheduled success sends a complete compact brief, later hourly checks send only for material changes, every sent update is self-contained, human time/contract/strategy labels replace raw protocol values, and existing Combo Yield positions keep their strategy attribution even when the current Combo Yield candidate list is empty.

Non-goals remain unchanged: no scheduler migration, no new heartbeat state machine, no strategy/ranking rewrite, no ledger migration, no provider-routing rewrite, and no production config edit.

The user confirmed this goal and requested Gateflow execution after approving the A+ plan and correcting the Combo Yield example.

## Revision 6 delta reviewed

Revision 6 adds one product invariant:

- candidate availability and position attribution are independent;
- `candidates.combo_yield=[]` means no new Combo Yield opening opportunity, not that an existing Combo Yield position loses its strategy family;
- the user projection may say `组合增强（Put 侧）` or `组合增强（Call 侧）`, but must not expose or parse raw `strategy_group_id` / `leg_role` strings.

## Direct code evidence

- `src/application/daily_decision_brief_service.py::_position_view` currently preserves `strategy_group_id` and `leg_role` but does not project an explicit normalized `strategy_family`; implementation must add the explicit field at the owning service boundary instead of making the renderer parse an ID.
- `src/application/daily_decision_brief_service.py::_close_action` already resolves strategy family from `strategy_family` / `strategy`, providing a compatible source rule for the position projection.
- Candidate payloads are already partitioned into `sell_put`, `covered_call`, and `combo_yield`, so an empty Combo candidate list can remain independent from position rendering.
- `src/application/daily_decision_brief_renderer.py` currently renders internal identities and raw lifecycle labels, confirming the planned allowlisted projection remains the correct owner for user-safe wording.

## Adversarial lenses

### Architecture boundary

**Pass.** The new invariant is owned by the service projection (`strategy_family` and normalized leg-side facts) and consumed by the renderer. The renderer is explicitly forbidden from parsing `strategy_group_id` or broker codes. Canonical audit identities remain unchanged.

### State/lifecycle compatibility

**Pass.** No action IDs, brief IDs, persisted schema versions, delivery keys, confirmation pointers, or historical artifacts are migrated. Adding explicit position projection fields is additive and does not alter action lifecycle identity.

### Optimality / overengineering

**Pass.** The fix does not add a group registry, new persisted entity, schema migration, or special Combo notification pipeline. It reuses structured close-advice facts already present in the assembler.

### Failure modes

- Missing or unknown strategy family must render a neutral position label, not infer from IDs.
- Missing leg role must show `组合增强` without inventing Put/Call side.
- Raw leg-role values must be allowlist-mapped; unknown values must not be echoed.
- Candidate-empty / existing-position-present must have a dedicated fixture.
