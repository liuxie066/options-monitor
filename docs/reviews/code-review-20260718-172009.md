## Findings

### ADR-01-已修复-[高]-Group topology trusts leg labels without validating option type, side or diagonal expiry order

- **入口/函数**: `build_strategy_attribution() -> _build_topology()`
- **文件(行号)**: `domain/domain/performance/strategy_attribution.py`
- **输入场景**: imported/repaired/legacy event carries `leg_role=funding_put` on a long Put or Call, or `participation_call` on a short Call, or a claimed diagonal group whose Call does not expire after the Put.
- **实际分支**: topology validates count, account, symbol, currency and multiplier only, then marks the group ready.
- **预期行为**: management attribution must fail closed unless Funding Put proves short Put, Participation Call proves long Call, and diagonal expiry ordering is valid.
- **实际行为**: mislabeled legs enter funding metrics and cycle PnL/capital under the wrong owner. A short Call could be described as premium-basis long-call capital; an invalid expiry structure could receive residual-tail semantics.
- **直接证据**: `_build_topology()` has no option_type/position_side/expiration checks despite the lifecycle design requiring them.
- **影响**: incorrect funding attribution, capital denominator and lifecycle classification for malformed historical/imported data.
- **建议改法和验证点**: validate role/type/side and, when structure is diagonal/staggered, `put.expiration < call.expiration`; invalid groups remain canonical totals but attribution partial. Add adversarial tests.
- **修复风险**: 低
- **严重程度**: 高
- **Gateflow finding decision**: accepted
- **Re-review state**: 已修复

### ADR-02-已修复-[中]-Group quality can report observed while its PnL or efficiency is partial

- **入口/函数**: `_group_report()`
- **文件(行号)**: `domain/domain/performance/strategy_attribution.py`
- **输入场景**: Call opening/end mark is missing, actual fee evidence is missing, or a PnL metric is partial while strategy metadata itself has no issue.
- **实际分支**: group quality is based only on `attribution_issues` from facts/segments.
- **预期行为**: group-level quality must reflect the material management metrics it exposes; consumers should not see an observed group beside partial PnL/efficiency.
- **实际行为**: `quality.status=observed` can coexist with `pnl.period_total_net.status=partial` and capital efficiency partial.
- **直接证据**: final quality expression checks only the locally collected metadata issue set.
- **影响**: downstream users can accept incomplete cross-period performance as complete.
- **建议改法和验证点**: aggregate group quality from metadata issues plus group/funding/participation/assigned-stock PnL and efficiency statuses; preserve metric-specific details. Add missing-mark and missing-fee tests.
- **修复风险**: 中
- **严重程度**: 中
- **Gateflow finding decision**: accepted
- **Re-review state**: 已修复
